POEM
Id: youji
Poet: 杜牧 Du Mu
Title: 有寄 / Sent to Someone

雲闊煙深樹，江澄水浴秋。
美人何處在，明月萬山頭。

LEXICON
有 yǒu, have; there is, exist
寄 jì, send; entrust, lodge
雲 (云) yún, cloud
闊 (阔) kuò, wide; broad, vast
煙 (烟) yān, mist; smoke, haze
深 shēn, deep; dense, far
樹 (树) shù, tree; plant
江 jiāng, river; *esp.* the Yangzi
澄 chéng, clear; limpid, still
水/氵 shuǐ, water; river
浴 yù, bathe; wash
秋 qiū, autumn
美 měi, beautiful; fine, admirable
人/亻 rén, person; people, others
何 hé, what; why, how, where
處 (处) chù, place; where; *read chǔ*, dwell
在 zài, be at; be present, exist
明 míng, bright; clear, illuminate
月 yuè, moon; month
萬 (万) wàn, ten thousand; countless, all
山 shān, mountain; hill
頭 (头) tóu, top; head, end

COMPOUNDS
美人 měirén, a beauty; *lit.* fair person, *often* a friend or patron longed for
何處 (何处) héchù, where; *lit.* what place

NOTES
Line 3: 美人, compound "a beauty," often a friend or patron longed for; 何處, compound "where"
