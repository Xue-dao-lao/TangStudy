POEM
Id: jingyesi
Poet: 李白 Li Bai
Title: 靜夜思 / Quiet Night Thoughts
Week: 1

床前明月光，疑是地上霜。
舉頭望明月，低頭思[故鄉]。

LEXICON
靜 (静) jìng, quiet; still, calm
夜 yè, night
床 chuáng, bed; couch; well-railing
前 qián, before; in front of; former
+明 míng, bright; clear, illuminate
+月 yuè, moon; month
光 guāng, light; radiance, glory
疑 yí, suspect; doubt, wonder whether
是 shì, be; this; right, correct
地 dì, ground; earth, land
+上 shàng, on; above, up; ascend
霜 shuāng, frost
舉 (举) jǔ, raise; lift; all, whole
頭 (头) tóu, head; top, end
望 wàng, gaze toward; look into the distance; hope; full moon
低 dī, lower; low
思 sī, think of; long for; thought
故 gù, old; former; reason, therefore
鄉 (乡) xiāng, village; home region, countryside

COMPOUNDS
故鄉 (故乡) gùxiāng, home; native place (*lit.* old village)

TRANSLATIONS
== Judith Gautier | L’auberge | 1867
Note: Selon Li Taï-Pé
Je me suis couché dans ce lit d’auberge;
la lune, sur le parquet, jetait une lueur blanche,
Et j’ai d’abord cru qu’il avait neigé sur le parquet.
J’ai levé la tête vers la lune claire, et j’ai songé aux pays que je vais parcourir et aux étrangers qu’il me faudra voir.
Puis j’ai baissé la tête vers le parquet, et j’ai songé à mon pays et aux amis que je ne verrai plus.

== Joseph Edkins | Thoughts on a Quiet Night | 1890
Before my couch the moon shines bright
Or is it the snow that reflects this light?
I see the moon when I raise my head
I look down and my thoughts are homeward led.

== Herbert Giles | Night Thoughts | 1898
I wake, and moonbeams play around my bed,
Glittering like hoar-frost to my wondering eyes;
Up towards the glorious moon I raise my head,
Then lay me down, — and thoughts of home arise.
