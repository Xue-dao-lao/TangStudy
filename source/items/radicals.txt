RADICALS
Id: radicals
Title: 部首 / Common Radicals

LEXICON
人/亻 rén, person; people, others
刀/刂 dāo, knife; blade
力 lì, strength; force, effort
又 yòu, again; also, moreover; *originally* a right hand
口 kǒu, mouth; opening, entrance
囗 wéi, enclosure; *radical only*, not the same as 口
土 tǔ, earth; soil, land
夕 xī, evening; dusk
大 dà, great; big
女 nǚ, woman; girl, daughter
子 zǐ, child; son, boy; master; *nominalizer*
寸 cùn, inch; a little
小 xiǎo, small; little, young
工 gōng, work; skill, artisan
幺 yāo, tiny; slight
弓 gōng, bow
心/忄 xīn, heart; mind, feelings
戈 gē, dagger-axe; weapon, war
手/扌 shǒu, hand
日 rì, sun; day
木 mù, tree; wood
水/氵 shuǐ, water; river
田 tián, field; farmland
目 mù, eye; look
示/礻 shì, show; *as a component*, spirits and rites
絲/糸 (丝) sī, silk; thread
耳 ěr, ear; *final particle*, only
衣/衤 yī, clothing; robe
言 yán, speech; words, say
貝 (贝) bèi, cowrie shell; treasure, money
走 zǒu, run; walk, go
足/⻊ zú, foot; enough
金/釒 jīn, metal; gold
門 (门) mén, gate; door
隹 zhuī, bird; *radical only*, short-tailed bird
雨 yǔ, rain
馬 (马) mǎ, horse
