RADICALS
Id: radicals
Title: 部首 / Common Radicals

LEXICON
人/亻, person; people, others
刀/刂, knife; blade
力, strength; force, effort
又, again; also, moreover; *originally* a right hand
口, mouth; opening, entrance
囗, enclosure; *radical only*, not the same as 口
土, earth; soil, land
夕, evening; dusk
大, great; big
女, woman; girl, daughter
子, child; son, boy; master; *nominalizer*
寸, inch; a little
小, small; little, young
工, work; skill, artisan
幺, tiny; slight
弓, bow
心/忄, heart; mind, feelings
戈, dagger-axe; weapon, war
手/扌, hand
日, sun; day
木, tree; wood
水/氵, water; river
田, field; farmland
目, eye; look
示/礻, show; *as a component*, spirits and rites
絲/糸 (丝), silk; thread
耳, ear; *final particle*, only
衣/衤, clothing; robe
言, speech; words, say
貝 (贝), cowrie shell; treasure, money
走, run; walk, go
足/⻊, foot; enough
金/釒, metal; gold
門 (门), gate; door
隹, bird; *radical only*, short-tailed bird
雨, rain
馬 (马), horse
