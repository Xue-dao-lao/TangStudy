POEM
Id: ganxing
Poet: 李端 Li Duan
Title: 感興 / Moved by a Feeling

[香爐]最高頂，中有[高人]住。
日暮下山來，月明上山去。

LEXICON
感 gǎn, be moved; feel, stir
興 (兴) xìng, mood; inspiration, delight; *read xīng*, rise, flourish
香 xiāng, fragrant; incense, fragrance
爐 (炉) lú, burner; stove, censer
最 zuì, most; highest
高 gāo, high; lofty, tall
頂 (顶) dǐng, summit; top, crown of the head
中 zhōng, middle; inside, among; *read zhòng*, hit the mark
有 yǒu, have; there is, exist
人/亻 rén, person; people, others
住 zhù, dwell; live, stay
日 rì, sun; day
暮 mù, dusk; evening, late
下 xià, descend; below, down, under
山 shān, mountain; hill
來 (来) lái, come; since; *after a verb*, toward the speaker
月 yuè, moon; month
明 míng, bright; clear, illuminate
上 shàng, ascend; on, above, up
去 qù, go; depart, leave

COMPOUNDS
香爐 (香炉) xiānglú, Incense Burner (Peak); *lit.* censer, *a peak of Mount Lu*
高人 gāorén, recluse; *lit.* lofty person, man of high character
